My lady,

I know sometimes I probably sound like a broken record when you've had a stressful day, or I bother you with my dumb fictional stories, or maybe there are moments when I make you feel like I don't care as much as I should.

But if there's one thing I wish I could make you understand, it's this:

You matter to me. More than I think I could ever properly put into words.

I care about your happiness. I care about your dreams, your worries, your little moments, and even the things you might think are too small to matter. I care about you—not just the version of you that's smiling and having a good day, but also the you that's tired, overwhelmed, frustrated, or doesn't know what to say.

I want to be someone you can come to when life gets heavy. Someone you can talk to when you need to let everything out. You don't always have to be okay around me. You don't have to have the right words. Sometimes you can just be there, and I'll still want to be there with you.

I don't think there's a perfect sentence that could ever explain how much I love you. I've tried, and I'll probably keep trying, because somehow every time I think I've said enough, I realize there's still more I want you to know.

You became someone incredibly important to me, and I'm grateful for every memory we've made, every conversation, every laugh, every little moment that somehow became something I'll remember.

And if I ever make you question how much I care, I hope you remember this:

I love you. I choose you. And I'll always want to be a safe place for you to put your heart.

So this little letter is just another way of saying what I probably don't say enough—

I love you, my angel. 🩷

Happy six months, my love. ❤️
